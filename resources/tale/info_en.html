Tale+ Setup Guide

A complete guide from purchase to activation

1. How to Purchase Tale+

1Start the Bot
Open Tale Bot
Open the link and tap "start" at the very bottom.
If the link says it's invalid, please install Telegram.
Need support?
2Open Menu
Tap "open menu" at the bottom left of the screen.
3Go to Purchase
Select "purchase" from the menu options.
4Add Balance
Tap "add balance" on the screen.
5Payment Method & Amount
Select Telegram Stars, choose "500", and complete your purchase.

2. Installing Tale

1Get the App
Tap the "Get" button in the top right corner.
2Open in Telegram
When asked "Open this page in Telegram?", tap "Open".
3Download the File
Tap the "↓ (Download)" button on the highlighted file to start downloading.
4Save to Files
Once the download is complete, open the share menu and select "Save to Files".
5Select Library
Select the "Library" tab in Ksign.
Don't have Ksign?
6Tap the Add Button
Tap the "+" button in the top right corner.
7Select Import Type
Select "import from files".
8Recents Tab
Choose "Recents" in the bottom right corner.
9Select the .ipa File
Select the Tale stars ipa file to import it.
10Select the App
Tap Tale from the added list.
11Start Signing
Tap "sign&install", then tap "Start signing" on the next screen. Wait for about 5 minutes.
12Complete
Once completed appears on the screen, verify the installation to finish.

3. Activation Steps

1Open MOD MENU
Launch the installed Tale stars and tap "MOD MENU" at the bottom left.
2View Key Information
Tap the glowing yellowish button inside the menu.
3Copy the Key
Tap the "COPY" button to copy your activation key.
4Bot Menu
Go back to the Tale Bot and tap "open menu" again.
5Return to Purchase
Select "Purchase".
6Select Activation Tab
Tap the "activation" tab located at the bottom of the screen.
7Paste the Key
Paste the copied key into the input field and tap the "activation" button to complete the setup!
